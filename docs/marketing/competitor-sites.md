## 2. Recurring patterns

**Structure.** The pitch-page apps (Collectr, CollX, ManaBox, CardCastle, TCG Album) share one order:
Hero with value line + download/signup → proof bar (rating, users) → features (scan, track, value, decks) → deep dive on the scanner → testimonials/creators → pricing → final CTA → footer with disclaimer. Pricing sits late, after proof. A FAQ appears only at TCG Album. A second group (Archidekt, Pokellector, Delver Lens) has no landing page at all: the database or the feed is the home page. A B2B/seller block shows up at CollX ("Card Dealer Pro") and CardCastle ("Merchant" tier with hardware), i.e. resellers are addressed in their own section, not in the hero.

**Imagery.** Phone mockups dominate (Collectr, CollX, ManaBox, CardCastle, TCG Album). Real card images appear only where a database backs the site (Archidekt, Pokellector) or as hero products (TCG Album: Pikachu-ex, Lugia-ex). Game logos are rare among apps (CardCastle only); the shop Gate to the Games uses them, but it resells licensed product, which is a different situation from an app. The strongest proof image is CardCastle's unedited, "not sped up" scan video.

**Tone.** Best: ManaBox "Get your cards together!" (cheerful, short, concrete); CollX "Snap a photo of your cards and get the value in seconds" (one verb, one promise, one time); TCG Album "Just point your camera at the card and review the result" (plain, honest). Worst for our goals: Collectr "Collect. Track. Profit." (the stock-market voice the brief rules out); Dragon Shield / Dex "the ultimate..." (empty superlative); Delver Lens "Delver Lens (DL) is a scanner for Magic the Gathering cards designed to organize your collection." (a definition, not a headline). Collectr does show that resellers respond to value framing; the trick is to say "what your cards are worth" without "portfolio" and "profit".

**CTA.** App store badges are the default primary CTA. Web-first products use one verb: "Sign Up", "Try the Scanner", "Start tracking today". TCG Album's no-signup scanner trial is the lowest-friction CTA in the set.

**Social proof.** Numbers right under the hero (Collectr 4.8★/100k reviews/10M users; ManaBox 4.8★/500K users), creators and influencers (Collectr, Ludex), awards (CollX), press logos (Delver Lens). Delver Lens's 200-version public changelog works as quiet proof of a maintained product.

**Disclaimers.** About half name the rights holder in a one-line footer notice (ManaBox, Archidekt, CardCastle, Pokellector, TCG Album). The rest show only a copyright line. Nobody who names four publishers does it well, because nobody covers four.

## 3. Gaps Voidbinder can own

- **Four games in one app.** Every app covers either one game (ManaBox, Delver, TCG Album, Pokellector) or "everything" generically (Collectr, TCGplayer). Pokémon + Yu-Gi-Oh! + Magic + One Piece, named, is open ground.
- **German cards, German operator.** Only TCG Album scans German cards, Pokémon only. No one says "Betreiber in Deutschland, Daten in der EU" on the page.
- **Offline scanner.** No competitor mentions offline scanning. Useful at fairs, in card shops with bad signal, at tournaments.
- **Both price sources.** Competitors show one: TCGplayer (US) or Cardmarket (TCG Album). Showing Cardmarket and TCGplayer side by side is a real reseller argument (arbitrage EU/US) without sounding like a trading desk.
- **Open source (AGPL) + cheap hosted tier.** Nobody in the set is open source. Moxfield/Archidekt prove collectors pay to support tools they trust (Patreon); "your data is yours, the code is public" is an honest trust signal.
- **Streamer Kit.** Collectr uses creators as advertising; nobody gives creators a tool (overlays for pack openings, live values). This also brings the streamers' audiences.
- **Honest scan proof.** TCGplayer's reviews criticise accuracy. An unedited scan video (CardCastle style) of German cards in sleeves would be hard to match.

## 4. Recommendation for voidbinder.de

**Structure (one page plus apply form).**

1. Hero: headline, one subline, primary CTA "Beta-Zugang anfragen / Apply for access", secondary "So funktioniert's". Short real scan clip on the right.
2. Four-game strip: game names as text (logos only after a trademark check; a nominative text mention is the safe default).
3. Three-step "Scan, sort, know the value".
4. Feature blocks: offline scanner · Cardmarket + TCGplayer prices · deck builder · collection/binder.
5. "For resellers": bulk scan, condition/printing, both price sources, export. Its own section, value language, no profit charts.
6. "For streamers": Streamer Kit preview.
7. "Open source, made in Germany": AGPL, EU hosting, hosted tier price.
8. Pricing (self-hosted free, hosted tier) → FAQ → apply form.
9. Footer: Impressum, Datenschutz, trademark notice naming all four rights holders, "Powered by Voidcom".

**Imagery.** Real card scans in sleeves and binders, ideally German prints, plus app screenshots. One unedited scan clip. Avoid candlestick/portfolio charts; show a price as a number with its source and date.

**Tone.** Informative and cheerful, du-form in German, one concrete fact per sentence. Value talk is fine ("what your cards are worth today on Cardmarket"); "profit", "portfolio", "invest" are not.
